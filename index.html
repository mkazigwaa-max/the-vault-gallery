STORY STAMPS — FILTER THE WALL:

These are not the most famous paintings in the world. They are the ones with the best stories behind them — the thefts, the feuds, the severed heads sent as legal documents, the models who nearly died for the calm on the surface. Each one hangs at its true relative size, with the detail nobody tells you marked on the canvas.
